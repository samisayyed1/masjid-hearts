The Prophet ﷺ said: "When a person dies, their deeds come to an end except for three: ongoing charity, knowledge that is benefited from, or a righteous child who prays for them" (Sahih Muslim 1631).

The Arabic for the first of these is **sadaqah jariyah**. *Jariyah* means flowing, like a stream. It is charity that keeps moving after the giver has stopped.

## What makes charity "ongoing"

Ordinary sadaqah is a meal for someone hungry today. It is rewarded, and then it is finished. Sadaqah jariyah is something that stays in use: a well people draw from for decades, a book that keeps being read, a tree that keeps giving fruit.

In another narration the Prophet ﷺ listed deeds that continue to reach a believer after death. Among them are knowledge that was taught, a copy of the Qur'an left behind, a house built for travellers, a stream made to flow, and **a masjid that was built** (Sunan Ibn Majah 242).

## Why a masjid is a clear example

Think about what happens inside one, every day, for as long as it stands:

- Every prayer offered in it.
- Every child who learns to read the Qur'an in it.
- Every person who finds their way back to Allah on its carpet.

We hope that those who helped build that masjid, and those who help keep it standing, share in the reward of the good done inside it.

## It does not have to be large

It is easy to assume this is for the wealthy. It is not. The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever builds a masjid for Allah, even one the size of a sandgrouse's nest or smaller, Allah will build for them a house in Paradise (Sunan Ibn Majah 738). No one prays in a masjid the size of a bird's nest. The image tells us that no contribution is too small to count.

A small amount given regularly is also loved by Allah. Aishah (may Allah be pleased with her) reported that the Prophet ﷺ said the most beloved deeds to Allah are those done consistently, even if they are small (Sahih al-Bukhari 6464, Sahih Muslim 783).

That is why Masjid Hearts is built around [small monthly gifts](/#give), pooled and shared across many masajid.
